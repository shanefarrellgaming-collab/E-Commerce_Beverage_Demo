07 / THE IMPORTANT STUFF

GOOD QUESTIONS.

Where does Interbev ship? +

Our business concept aims to serve international customers. Actual shipping destinations would depend on local import rules, product eligibility, and logistics partners. During this demo, all destinations are simulations, not guaranteed service areas.

How much is shipping? +

Checkout displays illustrative shipping based on your selected region. Final real-world rates would require carrier quotations and destination-specific import requirements.

Can I buy drinks without a box? +

Absolutely. Add any individual drinks to your bag and build your own custom selection.

Can I buy a box just once? +

Yes. Every size offers a one-time purchase or a monthly subscription in our demo.

Can I cancel a subscription? +

The proposed policy is cancellation before the next renewal. There are no live subscriptions on this student-project website.

What if drinks arrive damaged? +

Our proposed policy is to contact support within 7 days with an order number and photos. Verified damage would qualify for a replacement or refund. Actual terms are not in effect for this demo.

Can I return a drink I don't like? +

We propose accepting returns only for unopened, eligible goods where applicable law permits. Opened drinks generally cannot be returned for a change of mind. Faulty or misdescribed products would be handled under applicable consumer rights.

Is this a real store? +

No. Interbev is a university e-commerce project. Products, pricing, availability, reviews, subscription offerings, and shipping are demonstrations. No payments or orders are processed.
